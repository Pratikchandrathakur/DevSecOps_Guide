# Office Network, Bandwidth, and Power Continuity Plan

## Power Resilience
- UPS sizing per rack/core devices
- Generator integration where available
- Monthly battery health checks

## Bandwidth Planning
- Baseline utilization by office and VPN users
- QoS for critical services (VoIP/video/business apps)
- Burst capacity strategy with ISP

## Routing Resilience
- Dual WAN failover policy
- Health-based route switching
- Test failover quarterly
