# Vendor and ISP Management Playbook

## ISP Governance
- Primary + secondary ISP for redundancy
- SLA tracking: uptime, MTTR, packet loss, jitter
- Monthly service quality review

## Escalation Matrix
- L1 NOC
- L2 account manager
- L3 regional escalation

## Deliverables
- Contact roster
- Circuit inventory
- Renewal and cost tracking
- Incident RCA from vendor side
